About Pakistan Tourism

Land of Diversity and Hospitality

Pakistan is home to five of the world's 14 mountains taller than 8,000 meters, sprawling deserts, fertile plains, and a rich history stretching back to the Indus Valley Civilization. Whether you are looking for trekking adventures, cultural heritage, or peaceful family resorts, Pakistan has something unique for every traveler.
